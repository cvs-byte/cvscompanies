1. Overview

At CVS Companies ("we", "our", or "us"), accessible from https://cvscompanies.tech/, we prioritize the privacy and security of our visitors, clients, and partners. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect and how we utilize and protect it.

2. Information We Collect

When you interact with our website, request a quote, or contact us via email, phone, or WhatsApp, we may collect:

  • Contact Details: Your name, business email address, phone number, and company name.
  • Project Specifications: Descriptions of your technical needs, budget parameters, and business goals.
  • Technical & Usage Data: Standard server logs, IP addresses, browser types, referring pages, and device information to optimize website performance.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data solely for legitimate business purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain our web development and IT services.
  • To respond to your inquiries and deliver customized proposals.
  • To communicate project updates, maintenance notices, and technical support.
  • To maintain website security and prevent spam or unauthorized access.

4. Data Protection & Confidentiality

We enforce strict technical and organizational safeguards to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, or alteration of your personal data. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties or advertising brokers.

5. Client Code & Intellectual Property

All client code, database records, and proprietary business documents shared with CVS Companies under contractual engagements are treated as strictly confidential and protected by non-disclosure standards.

6. Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses minimal, necessary cookies to ensure basic security, session integrity, and responsive layout performance. We do not deploy intrusive third-party cross-site behavioral trackers.
