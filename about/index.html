Who We Are

CVS Companies is a specialized technology and digital solutions firm based in Tirupati, Andhra Pradesh, India. We empower businesses to thrive in a digital-first economy by building resilient web applications, mobile platforms, enterprise software, and automated cloud workflows.

Founded by Cherukuri Vastav Sekar (CEO & AWS Certified Developer – Associate), our core philosophy is rooted in technical honesty, clean code, transparent pricing, and direct communication. Rather than deploying over-bloated, generic templates, we engineer bespoke digital products designed to meet specific business outcomes with maximum speed and security.

Our Core Mission: To demystify complex software engineering and cloud infrastructure for growing organizations, providing reliable, high-performance digital tools that scale without unexpected maintenance headaches.
